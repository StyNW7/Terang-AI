**Terang AI** (PT Terang Inovasi Indonesia) is an Indonesian AI-powered EdTech startup (2024) that grew from B2C (civil-service and university entrance prep, scholarships, AI interview simulation) into B2B. Its two B2B products — **AI Candidate Interview** (HRTech) and **Terang Events** (EventTech) — are both promising, but the core team has only **7 people**. This repository contains a **web prototype**, a **survey analysis (n = 113)**, and the two strategies our proposal paper tests side by side:

| | Strategy | Core idea |
|:--:|---|---|
| **PRO** | **ONE** — *Focused B2B Growth* | Focus on AI Candidate Interview, prove its value, then expand. |
| **CONTRA** | **ACE** — *Two Products, One Coordinated Growth Engine* | Develop AI Candidate Interview + Terang Events in parallel, selectively. |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Independent academic case study for **BCDC 2026** by team **Para Ketua OK** — not an official Terang AI product or announcement. All prices, targets and financial figures are **proposals and assumption-based projections**. The prototype runs on **fictional data** with no real AI model, payments or personal data.

## ◆ Background & Problem

With limited team capacity and budget, Terang AI faces a dilemma: **concentrate resources on one product or develop both in parallel**. The root-cause analysis identifies three core challenges:

| Challenge | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **Resource Capacity Gap** | 7 people (CEO, CPO, 3 Product, 2 Strategy & Ops) must serve two products with different needs. |
| **Product-Market Validation Gap** | Each product's commercial viability is not yet proven through adoption, revenue and cost efficiency. |
| **Portfolio Execution Complexity** | Two markets, two sales channels and two service models add coordination overhead. |

**Market opportunity:** 4,303 active higher-education institutions in Indonesia (Kemdiktisaintek, 2026) for Terang Events, and 20% of companies already using AI in recruitment (Jobstreet survey of 1,273 recruiters) for AI Candidate Interview. Compared with competitors focused on one side (Cynchrony & Intereval for AI interviews, GOERS for events/ticketing), Terang AI stands out with **two complementary products** already in real use: **10,000+ AI interview sessions** and a competition with **347+ participants**.

**Survey evidence (Google Form, n = 113)** — details in [`data-analysis/`](data-analysis):

| Finding | Result | Supports |
|---|:--:|:--:|
| Prepared for an interview in the last 2 years | **91%** | ONE |
| Need answer feedback, yet few already practise with AI | **73% → 13%** | ONE |
| Organizers still using Google Forms / WhatsApp for registration | **80%** | ACE |
| Have both interview **and** event experience | **83%** | ACE |

<sub>Respondents are end users (candidates & event committees), not B2B buyers — the survey shows need, not willingness to pay.</sub>

## ◆ ONE Strategy — PRO

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/O.N.E-Focus%2C%20Prove%20Value%2C%20Then%20Expand-4A90E2?style=for-the-badge" alt="O.N.E — Focus, Prove Value, Then Expand">

Prioritises **AI Candidate Interview** (built on the Professor Terra technology, 10,000+ sessions) until it has measurable value, paying customers and a viable business model. Terang Events keeps running in maintenance mode.

| Pillar | Program | Key contents |
|---|---|---|
| **O** — Optimize Product Value | AI Interview Intelligence Suite | Role-Based Interview Builder, Evidence-Linked Assessment Report, Responsible AI & Privacy Controls |
| **N** — Navigate Market Growth | Pilot-to-Paid Growth Engine | HR Efficiency Clinic, Terang Pilot Challenge, Verified Impact Stories; targeting recruitment agencies & companies with high entry-level hiring |
| **E** — Evaluate for Expansion | ONE Growth Intelligence & Expansion Gate | Evaluates Product Value, Market Validation, Financial Sustainability, Operational Readiness → **Scale / Improve / Hold** decision |

**Pilot benchmarks:** 3–5 pilot companies, ≥ 40% paid conversion, recruiter satisfaction ≥ 4/5, ≥ 25% less administrative time, ≥ 40% contribution margin.

**12-month implementation** — 80% of capacity on AI Candidate Interview, 20% on maintenance & operations:

| Q1 (M1–M3) | Q2 (M4–M6) | Q3 (M7–M9) | Q4 (M10–M12) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Build & Validate | Pilot & Improve | Convert & Monetize | Evaluate & Scale |
| Priority features, AI quality testing | 6 pilots, 3 active customers | 16 pilots, 9 active customers | 24 pilots, 20 contracts, **18 active customers** |

**Proposed B2B pricing:** Starter Rp3.9M/month (100 sessions) · Growth Rp9.9M/month (350 sessions) · Enterprise Rp20.9M/month (800 sessions), plus a one-off onboarding fee.

## ◆ ACE Strategy — CONTRA

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/A.C.E-Two%20Products%2C%20One%20Growth%20Engine-EB657B?style=for-the-badge" alt="A.C.E — Two Products, One Growth Engine">

Develops **AI Candidate Interview and Terang Events in parallel, selectively**, to ride the AI-adoption momentum (47% annual growth among Indonesian businesses — AWS, 2025), keep the assets already built (~10,000 users, 3 B2B clients in 2025) and reduce dependence on a single market.

| Pillar | Program | Key contents |
|---|---|---|
| **A** — Agile Feature Development | ACE Dual-Product Innovation Sprint | *Events:* Smart Event Templates, Organizer Insight Dashboard, AI Event Assistant · *Interview:* Role-Based Interview, Evidence-Based Assessment, HR Review Dashboard. At most 1 major feature per product per sprint. |
| **C** — Coordinate Markets & Capacity | Dual-Market Growth Engine + ACE Resource Control Dashboard | *Events:* campuses, organizers, professional associations · *Interview:* companies & recruitment agencies. The dashboard tracks workload, budget, progress and priorities. |
| **E** — Evaluate and Evolve Portfolio | ACE Portfolio Intelligence | 6 aspects: adoption, conversion, retention, financial, efficiency, product quality; Monthly Product & Portfolio Review; AI fairness aligned with NIST AI RMF. |

**18-month implementation** — 3.15 FTE Interview · 2.65 FTE Events · 1.20 FTE shared operations:

| Q1 | Q2 | Q3 | Q4 | Q5 | Q6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Align & Build | Pilot & Activate | Acquire & Optimize | Expand Selectively | Improve Portfolio | Evaluate & Evolve |

**M18 targets:** 15 active Interview companies, 21 active organizers, 53 paid events, 24 completed Interview pilots.

**Monetisation:** Interview tiers same as ONE; Terang Events uses a platform fee (Starter Rp750K · Professional Rp2M · Institutional Rp5M + 2.5% of GMV), **Managed Event Boost** (Rp1.6M/campaign) and **Organizer Pro** (Rp1.5M/month).

## ◆ Comparison & Conclusion

Both strategies use the **same 7 people** (*controlled comparison*); only the implementation length differs.

| | ONE — PRO | ACE — CONTRA |
|---|--:|--:|
| Products | AI Candidate Interview | Interview + Terang Events |
| Main implementation | 12 months | 18 months |
| Revenue / cost during implementation | Rp833.2M / Rp1.78B | Rp1.47B / Rp3.20B |
| Projection horizon | 36 months | 48 months |
| Cumulative revenue | Rp8.45B | Rp15.71B |
| Cumulative surplus | Rp1.80B *(operating)* | Rp2.55B *(after tax)* |
| ROI on total costs | 27.1% | 20.5% |
| NPV (15% discount) | +Rp676.6M | +Rp701.7M |
| Annual IRR | 48.0% | 32.5% |
| Payback period | ~28.7 months | ~38.3 months |
| Max funding need (+20% reserve) | Rp1.44B | Rp2.21B |

<sub>B = billion, M = million (IDR). All figures are assumption-based projections; horizons and surplus definitions differ, so they are indicative.</sub>

> **Conclusion:** **ONE** fits if Terang AI wants **faster payback with lower risk**. **ACE** delivers **larger long-term profit and diversified revenue**, at the cost of higher upfront capital and slower payback. The final call is a managerial decision for Terang AI's CEO, Founder and team.

## ◆ Prototype Features

- **Public site:** landing, company profile, products, Strategy Lab, ONE & ACE pages per pillar, references.
- **AI Candidate Interview dashboard:** vacancies, Role-Based Interview Builder, candidates, evidence-based reports, Responsible AI, candidate portal.
- **Terang Events dashboard:** event creation (6-step wizard), Smart Event Templates, Repeat Event Planner, participants, judging, analytics, finance.
- **Super Admin:** ONE vs ACE financial simulator, Capacity & Operations (ACE Resource Control), Portfolio & Decision Gate.
- **15-slide presentation mode**, command palette (`Ctrl/⌘ + K`), light/dark theme, ID/EN language.

**Tech stack:** Next.js 16 · React 19 · TypeScript 5 · Tailwind CSS 4 · Zustand 5 · Recharts 3 · Zod 4. No backend, database or AI API — all fictional demo data lives in `localStorage`. The survey analysis uses Python & Jupyter (pandas, SciPy, scikit-learn, matplotlib).

## ◆ Responsible AI

- **Human-in-the-loop:** AI assists, humans decide — no automatic accept/reject button.
- **Evidence-based:** every rating links to a rubric and quotes from the candidate's answers.
- **Privacy & data security:** candidate consent, role-based access control and data retention windows.
- **Fairness:** assessment validity and bias monitoring are mandatory before any expansion.
